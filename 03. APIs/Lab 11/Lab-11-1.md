Agile Methods and Scrum

1.
Developement works better with iterative process which is open to implementing changes and feedback, rather than at the end.
Project requirements are set at the start of project, and wont be able to be validated until project finish.

2.
"Responding to change over following a plan"
learning about a better way to implement a feature during a sprint makes the agile team able to adapt by adding proposed changes to the backlog and implement in future sprint.

"Customer collaboration over contract negotiation"
Ongoing showcase and conversation with the customer makes adaption to input and customers need fluid.

5.
Sprint Review
    Scrum Team and stakeholders.
    Second to last step of the sprint. 
    Focus on product. 
    Show result and get feedback.
    Adjust backlog if needed.

Sprint Retrospective
    Scrum Team
    Last step of the sprint.
    Focus on the team. What went well and what did not during the sprint.
    Evaluation of collaboration methods, tooling and processes. 
    What to improve for next sprint.
